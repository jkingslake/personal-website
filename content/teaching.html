At Columbia University (2016–2026) my teaching ranged from introductory geology to graduate-level glaciology classes.

At Imperial College London I will teach a new three-week intensive glaciology course, starting in May 2027:

Glaciology
Graduate — Imperial College London · Spring 2027 (planned) · intensive three-week format

Physics of glaciers and ice sheets: ice flow, mass balance, hydrology, calving, sliding, and ice-sheet response to climate forcing, with hands-on computer practicals in which students build and examine glaciological models and analyze data. This course was originally developed and taught at Columbia; the Imperial version will be delivered intensively over three weeks. All course materials are available on GitHub.

I am happy to speak to school and college groups about glaciology and polar fieldwork — please get in touch.
