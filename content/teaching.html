At Columbia University (2016–2026) I taught introductory solid-Earth science, a graduate glaciology course, and a paper-reading seminar in glacial processes.

Glaciology
Graduate — Imperial College London · Spring 2027 (planned) · intensive three-week format

Physics of glaciers and ice sheets: flow laws, mass balance, subglacial hydrology, calving, and ice-sheet response to climate forcing, with hands-on computer practicals in which students build and analyse glaciological models. This course was originally developed and taught at Columbia; the Imperial version will be delivered intensively over three weeks. All course materials are available on GitHub.

I am happy to speak to school and college groups about glaciology and polar fieldwork — please get in touch.
