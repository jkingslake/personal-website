My teaching at Columbia spans solid-Earth science, glaciology, and quantitative Earth science methods.

Introduction to Solid Earth System Science (EESC 4220)
Undergraduate & graduate — Columbia University · 2016–2023 · up to 70 students

A broad introduction to solid-Earth processes including plate tectonics, seismology, volcanology, geomorphology, and the cryosphere, with emphasis on quantitative reasoning and real-world data.

Glaciology
Graduate — Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory / DEES · 2018–2023 · 6–14 students

Physics of glaciers and ice sheets: flow laws, mass balance, subglacial hydrology, calving, and ice-sheet response to climate forcing. The course alternates between lectures and hands-on computer practicals in which students build and analyse glaciological models.

Glacial Processes Seminar (EESC 9815)
Graduate — Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory · 2017

A discussion-based paper-reading seminar exploring classic and contemporary literature in glaciology, ice-sheet dynamics, and paleoglaciology. Students led weekly discussions on key papers and presented their own emerging research.
